Privacy policy

What we store

When you build a page, your profile, links, theme and page settings are saved in your browser (localStorage key linkey.state.v1). If you sign in with Google, the published version of that page is also stored in our database so it can be served at its public URL. Contacts, products, scheduled posts, auto-reply rules and short links are kept on this device only.

Public pages

A published page is readable by anyone with its URL. Treat everything on it as public. Links you publish are visible to visitors, and any third-party site you link to has its own privacy practices.

Analytics

Click and view counters shown in the dashboard are stored locally. This build does not load third-party analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and does not set advertising cookies.

Cookies and local storage

We use local storage for functionality: keeping you signed in, remembering preferences, and caching your page so the editor opens quickly. Sign-in is handled by Google and our authentication provider, which set the session token stored in this browser. Clearing site data removes it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, delete or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Contact vinaysuthar113@gmail.com to exercise them. You can delete your page and its database row with Profile → Delete account.

Retention and international transfers

Your published page stays in our database until you delete it from your profile; deleting your account removes it. Local data stays until you clear it. Our sign-in and database provider is Supabase, and Google handles the sign-in step; both process data outside your country.

Children

Linkey is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
